Green Roof Prototype

EGRID → AV building footprints (Bodenbedeckung «Gebäude») → vegetation index on the SWISSIMAGE 10 orthophoto → green-roof polygon & area. Runs fully in the browser (LV95 / EPSG:2056).

NIR status: the published SWISSIMAGE 10 tiles (up to the 2025 flight year) are 3-band RGB — true NDVI needs the near-infrared band expected with the 2026 release (not yet on STAC). Until then the prototype uses an RGB vegetation index (GLI/VARI) as a stand-in and switches to NDVI automatically as soon as a 4-band tile (or a local 4-band GeoTIFF, e.g. a SWISSIMAGE RS extract) is loaded.

Prototype caveats
  • An orthophoto index sees vegetation over the footprint — trees overhanging a roof count as green; shaded or dry/brown vegetation may fall below the threshold.
  • GLI/VARI are RGB stand-ins and noisier than NDVI (JPEG compression artifacts included) — treat results as indicative until the 2026 NIR band is available.
  • Areas are pixel counts × pixel area on LV95 (10 cm ⇒ 0.01 m²/px); building areas are exact planar polygon areas. GeoJSON export is WGS84 via swisstopo approximate formulas (~1 m).
  • «Gebäude» is the AV roof-projection footprint clipped to the parcel — buildings straddling the boundary are counted only inside it.
